Backend measurement and recommendation notes:
1. Owned assessment results are scoped to a versioned attempt and use server-created exercise-session results.
2. Assessment level is bounded by both average validated WPM and comprehension; speed alone cannot produce an advanced level.
3. Age-group assessment attempts use the student's active age-group configuration and its active admin assessment template when available.
4. Recommended training programs are active, non-deleted, non-assessment templates and are filtered by the student's age group when known.
5. Retention assessment is held until its minimum waiting window has elapsed; the API returns the future availability time.
